# PART 1 — THE API ROUTES (the half that is usually skipped)
📌 **Six routes. For each: what to send, what the right answer is, and what a WRONG answer looks like.**
🔑 **The admin routes and the teacher routes are the SAME ACT with two callers. The whole point of the test is that they agree.**

# PART 2 — F: a NOT-YET-STARTED course takes an absence FREE
**Door: `PATCH /api/bookings/:id/status` on a session of a course that has not started.**

### 2.1 The happy path
▶️ **Take a course whose first session is in the future and nothing has been taught. Declare one session absent.**
✅ **RIGHT: it is accepted, a make-up appears, and the course's LEAVE COUNT DOES NOT MOVE.** 🔑 **That last clause is the whole feature: free means the allowance is untouched.**
📌 **Check the count on the course screen before and after. 🚫 No query needed.**

### 2.2 The cap
▶️ **Keep declaring until it refuses.**
✅ **RIGHT: it refuses at the number of leave days THAT course bought, and the refusal NAMES the count (e.g. "4/4").**
🔴 **WRONG: a bare refusal with no numbers · a cap that is the same on every course regardless of what was bought.**

### 2.3 🔴 A COPY FINDING I am handing over with it — **expect this one, it is mine to fix**
**The refusal currently ends with words to the effect of "…or an admin can unlock it."** 🔴 **There is no unlock on this path.** ⇒ **The sentence offers a remedy that does not exist.**
▶️ **Tanya should READ the refusal and report the wording as she finds it.** 📌 **The reword is mine (copy is Team A's this batch) and goes to the owner with the batch. 🚫 She is not reporting a bug; she is confirming which words shipped.**

### 2.4 ⚠️ Two consequences the owner has been TOLD about — **so they are not reported as faults**
1. **Declaring free days does NOT reduce the ordinary leaves available later.** ⇒ **a course can reach TWICE its allowance in total absences.** ✅ **Deliberate; the owner has the sentence.**
2. **A declaration TAKEN BACK still uses up one of the free days.** ✅ **Also deliberate and also with him.**
🔑 **If Tanya meets either and it reads badly to her as a user, THAT is worth reporting — but as a judgement, not a defect.**

### 2.5 🔴 The dangerous one: a START-DATE CHANGE
▶️ **On a course that has NOT started: declare one absence free, take one ordinary leave, then CHANGE THE COURSE'S START DATE by a week.**
✅ **RIGHT: each session keeps what it was — the free one stays free, the charged one stays charged.**
🔴 **WRONG: either one changes into the other.** 🔑 **This is the case I most expect to break, because moving the start date moves sessions across the "has it started" line.** 📌 **Pinned in code; never yet driven against a real database.**
