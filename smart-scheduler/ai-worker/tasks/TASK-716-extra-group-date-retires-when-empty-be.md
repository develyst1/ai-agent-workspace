## 1 · The empty extra date — what the code does today (the defect)
**A GROUP row with no live seat stays a live CONFIRMED class:** it holds the coach's slot (only a Private booked into it makes it step aside, `yieldEmptyGroupSlot`), it is on the coach's daily reminder and weekly schedule (the reminder lists a group row whether or not it has seats), and 713 drew the coach's freelance hour when it confirmed it. ⇒ **A 713 extra date whose seats all leave = a coach told to teach an empty class.** For an ordinary group date this is pre-existing (a series can sit empty before enrolment, and that is legitimate). **For a date that exists ONLY to carry make-ups, it is our defect** — 713 creates the date, so 713's date must go when its reason goes.

## 2 · A CLOSED series — what CLOSED means (read in code)
**CLOSED = "no new dates, no new enrolment; existing rows untouched — a closed series still runs out its dates"** (`schema.ts` `group_closed_at`; `GROUP_SERIES_CLOSED` "กลุ่มนี้ปิดรับแล้ว"). **It is NOT ended.** The extra date carries the series' OWN children (no new enrolment), and each family and the coach are TOLD the new date (born-CONFIRMED messages) ⇒ **not a class nobody expects. 713's behaviour stands.**
**One gap to close:** "the stamp is on every row of the key" is the series' invariant, and 713's insert does not stamp the new row ⇒ the screen's `closedAt` reads null on that one row. **Stamp `group_closed_at` on the extra row when the series is closed** (the same timestamp). `assertGroupSeriesOpen` already reads ANY row, so nothing reopens today — this keeps the invariant true.
