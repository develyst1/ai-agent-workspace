## §1 Verified — your read is right, and it is only HALF the reason Khwan sees English
1. **The per-user link is the pin, exactly as you read it.** `linkRichMenuToUser` is called from `settleLinkedRole` (account-link) and the language toggle only; `setDefaultRichMenu` moves the channel default, which LINE serves **only to a follower with no per-user link**. 2. 🔴 **The second cause — `knownEN` is never published, and the link that wants it fails SILENTLY.** `settleLinkedRole` calls `linkRoleRichMenu(role, lang)` and then, for a customer, `linkKnownRichMenu(lang)`. `linkKnownRichMenu` reads `ids.knownEN` for an EN user — and `publishRichMenus` creates **six** menus, `knownEN` not among them (TASK-247 §4, deliberate: "a stored id with no uploaded image renders BLANK"). `NAME_TO_KEY` says the same. This is the exact shape of the bug the file's own TASK-247 note describes ("`linkKnownRichMenu` then found no `knownTH`, and — best-effort by design — did nothing, silently").
   ⇒ **§2.3's answer, both ways:** if her `line_lang` is **EN**, the sweep re-links her to the CURRENT `parentEN` (correct by today's rules — and she will still never see the 6-cell "known" menu, because no EN one exists); if it is **TH**, the sweep re-links her to `knownTH` and she gets the owner's orange menu.
