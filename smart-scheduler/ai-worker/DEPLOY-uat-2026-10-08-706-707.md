### For ADMINS setting up a CAMP — `TASK-707` (front) · REQ-116
**Saving a camp day now sends every coach's hours WHOLE:** a coach with their OWN hours keeps them on any save; a coach on the day's hours stays on them (and follows a change to the day's hours); a half window is never sent. ⇒
- **Adding a coach no longer resets the others' own hours** — so no more refusal naming ANOTHER coach (Khwan's "Kowjoe → ครู Bank 13:00");
- **Moving a coach to hours that share one end with what they show** (10–15 → 13–15) **saves** — no more *"ต้องระบุทั้งเวลาเริ่มและเวลาจบ"*;
- 🔴 **and no more SILENT widening** of own-hours coaches to the full day on saves that did go through.
No new words. No server change.
⚠️ **The refusal *"วันที่ … ครู X มีคาบแล้ว"* can still appear — when it is TRUE:** the coach really holds another class in those hours, **or is on another camp week on the same dates** (uat has three overlapping weeks on 12 Oct — "12-16 Oct" OPEN, "12 -16 Oct" CLOSED, "12-13 Oct" CLOSED). **Which of those weeks are real is Khwan's decision.**
