## 3. The R1.4 pay period (money-grade — replaces the demo's calendar month)
- **R1.4:** a period runs from the **last day of the previous month** to the **day before the last day of this month** (*"30/9–30/10"*). NOT a calendar month.
- **Provenance — the one apparent contradiction is resolved, in favour of this (S15), no change (checked 2026-11-08, CUSTOMER-ANSWERS.md "Salary: slip, cycle & confirm"):** Khwan's 10-14 answers held two phrasings — **S15** "ตัดรอบ … สิ้นเดือน-1 วันก่อนสิ้นเดือน e.g. 30/9-30/10" (start = last day of prev month) and **QS2** "วันที่ 1 ถึงวันก่อนสิ้นเดือน แล้ววันสุดท้ายยกไปรอบเดือนถัดไป" (reads as start = the 1st). **Settled by the 10-15 draft-1:** the spec draft's §1.4 showed the S15 model concretely — "รอบ ต.ค. = 30/9 – 30/10", "กดยืนยันภายใน 31/10 12:00" (`project-docs/customer-2026-10-14-backoffice-spec-draft/backoffice-spec-draft.html:211,215,218`) — and she answered **"1.4 รอบเดือนและการยืนยัน: ตรง"** (`…/khwan-answers-draft1-VERBATIM.md §1.4`). QS2's "วันที่ 1" is reconciled by its own carry rule ("the last day carries to the next cycle" ⇒ each cycle begins on the prev month's last day = S15). So R1.4/this §3/`payPeriodRange` are correct as written; **nothing to Khwan.**
- **Schema (additive on `bo.pay_period`, link 1):** add `period_start` (date) + `period_end` (date). `year`/`month` stay as the human label. A helper `payPeriodRange(year, month)` computes `[period_start, period_end]` per R1.4. The hours reader and every auto line sum over `[period_start, period_end]` inclusive.
- **Lock (R1.5) unchanged** from SPEC-100: confirm by the last day of the month before 12:00 Asia/Bangkok; the lazy auto-lock stays. (The `lock_at` default already = last day 12:00 Bangkok; that is the confirm day, one day after `period_end` — correct.)

## 5. Link 2 — the hours reader (cuttable; the big independent module — a 2nd BE can build it in parallel with link 1)
A **read-only** module in backoffice-back that, given a `profile` (or teacher_id) and a period `[start,end]`, returns **per calendar day**: the **teaching hours by `class_type`** (private/duo/group/eca/camp), from the scheduling timetable, **including ECA and camp**. Non-teaching OT hours are NOT here (admin-entered, link 3).
- **Output contract (stable — links 1 & 3 depend only on this). Unit is FIXED: integer minutes everywhere (no float, no ambiguity):**
  `readHours(teacherId, start, end) → [{ date, byType: { private, duo, group, eca, camp }, teachingMinutesTotal }]`
  - `date` is the local (Asia/Bangkok) calendar date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Each `byType.<class>` is **integer minutes** taught in that class type that day; `teachingMinutesTotal` = their sum (integer minutes). A day with no teaching may be omitted or returned with all-zero — the TASK documents which; link 3 treats a missing day as zero.
  - **Resolved 2026-11-08 (Porter's seam question): the field is `teachingMinutesTotal`, NOT `teachingHoursTotal`.** The unit mismatch is gone — minutes in, and link 3 converts to hours only at the rule (e.g. Extra Hour thresholds, OT), rounding there, never in the reader.
- **Source (brownfield — the TASK's step 0 maps it, do NOT guess in the SPEC):** the lesson/session rows in `smart-scheduler-back`'s schema carry a teacher, a date, a `startTime`/`endTime` (duration), and a kind (PRIVATE/DUO/GROUP) with ECA/Camp via the `otherKind` booking code. The reader reads these **read-only** (no write, no FK). Cancelled/again-type rows excluded per the timetable's own status rules (the TASK step 0 states which statuses count). **This is the spine's hardest piece and its correctness is the slip's correctness** — it needs its own test fixtures of real timetable shapes.
- **No collision:** read-only; it does not edit any scheduler file's logic, only SELECTs.
- **R1.6 extension (student names — the "who was taught" itemisation, Khwan §1.3 "ขอรายละเอียดว่าเป็นน้องอะไร แค่ชื่อค่ะ"):** link 3 and the slip/PDF itemise each teaching line by class type today; R1.6 wants the **student name(s)** per lesson. The reader is extended to also return, per teaching day, the students taught — shape proposal: alongside `byType`, a `students: Array<{ classType, names: string[] }>` (names read-only from the booking/student rows; `is_system` excluded; a GROUP lists its live seats' students). Exact source + shape is the reader TASK's step-0 call (Sector cuts it for Brew against this, as with TASK-872) — it must stay **read-only**, integer-minute `byType` unchanged, and link 3 consumes `students` only for labels (never money). ## 6. Link 3 — the automatic pay lines (cuttable after links 1–2 land; it integrates both)
On the pay-run (SPEC-100 `runPeriod`), for each active profile, compute these **auto** lines (`source='auto'`, idempotent per (slip, kind, source_ref) once source_ref lands) over `[period_start, period_end]`, each **itemised per R1.6**:
- **FL teaching pay** (FL role only — `roleOfDay(date)==='FL'`): Σ over FL days and class types of `byType.<class>(date) ÷ 60 × fl_rate(profile, class_type)` (rate is per hour; reader is minutes; compute in satang, round at the line). Itemise per date with who was taught (R1.6).
  - **UI label = "Freelance Hour"** on the Payment-details / slip screen (REQ-5010 item 4, พี่ฟีน 2026-11-08, owner ruled "free, fold in"). This is the **display label only**; the internal `pay_line_kind` and the computation above are unchanged. Link 3's TASK carries this label.
  - **UPDATED 2026-11-09 (TASK-903): fl_pay is emitted ONE LINE PER (date × class type)**, each carrying a `class_type` field (a new nullable `bo.pay_class_type` column on `bo.pay_slip_line`, set only for fl_pay), so the slip/export split "Freelance Teaching Pay · <Class>" to match the final UI. sourceRef becomes `fl:${date}:${classType}` (idempotent per class). Totals are unchanged (the per-class amounts sum to the old per-date figure). Extra Hour / OT / Merit lines keep `class_type` NULL.
- **Extra Hour 350฿/hr** (FT·PT; on PT-day classes for PT): **per day**, teaching time **incl. ECA/camp** from the reader (`teachingMinutesTotal`), charged from the **5th** hour on a weekday, the **6th** on a weekend. `extraMinutes(day) = max(0, teachingMinutesTotal(day) − thresholdMin(day))`, `thresholdMin` = 240 (weekday, first 4 h) / 300 (weekend, first 5 h); pay = `extraMinutes ÷ 60 × 350` (compute in satang, round at the line). Itemise per date ("14/10 = 6 Hr", R1.6). Confirm the threshold against a hand example (SPEC §10).
- **OT** (FT·PT): `base_salary ÷ 26 ÷ 8 × 1.15` per hour × **hours entered by an admin** (non-teaching; NOT from the reader). A manual OT-hours input per slip per day; compute in satang, round at the line. 
- **Merit:** amount typed (teachers by Admin Poo q3m; staff by Khwan/ฟีน monthly), only where `merit_eligible`. Manual line; no auto compute.
- **Idempotency:** a re-run must not duplicate an auto line → needs `source_ref` + the partial-unique index (SPEC-100 §9b; it will be added in the migration that introduces auto-lines, i.e. link 3's or a shared one — link 3 TASK owns it).
