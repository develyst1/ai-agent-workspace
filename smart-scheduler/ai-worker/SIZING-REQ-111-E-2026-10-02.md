## §1 — What each of the three controls ACTUALLY does today (the read you asked for)
**All three live in one dialog** (`front .../OtherSeries/OtherSeriesDialogs.tsx` → `TeacherDialog`, `mode: "add" | "remove" | "swap"`) **and hit one service** (`back src/services/other-series.service.ts`).
📌 **First, the fact that explains everything below:** **a series row stores its PRIMARY teacher in `bookings.teacher_id` (+ `bookings.teacher_rate_minor`), and every OTHER teacher as a row in `booking_teachers` (+ its own `rate_minor`).** ⇒ 🔑 **"primary" is not a flag on a list of teachers — it is a different storage location.** *That single fact is the whole of item E.*

| | **1. Swap + "this session only"** | **2. Swap + "from here on"** | **3. Add teacher** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rows written** | **ONE live row on that date** — `bookings.teacher_id` **and** `teacher_rate_minor` | **every live row from that date on** — `bookings.teacher_id` **only** | **one `booking_teachers` row per target row** (`teacher_id` + `rate_minor`) |
| **Does the series' own teacher change?** | 🚫 **No** — the template's teacher is whoever holds the earliest live row; one later row moving does not move it | ⚠️ **Effectively YES** — from that date on every row names the new teacher, so the series reads as theirs | 🚫 **No** |
| **Original teacher: removed, or joined?** | **REMOVED from that session** — *"A covers for B", and only A is paid* | **REMOVED from every row from that date** | 🔴 **merely JOINED by a second — B is still on the session** |
| **Whose rate does the session end up at?** | ✅ **the INCOMING teacher's** — the given `rateMinor`, else the rate they already hold anywhere in this series (`seriesRateOf`), else 🚫 **REFUSED** (`RATE_REQUIRED`) — never the covered teacher's | 🔴 **the OUTGOING teacher's.** **`set({ teacherId })` leaves `teacher_rate_minor` untouched** ⇒ **the new primary inherits the old one's stored rate, silently** | **each teacher keeps their own `rate_minor`; the primary's is unchanged** |

### 🔴 Two findings from that table, independent of item E
1. **A "from here on" swap pays the new teacher at the OLD teacher's rate.** **It is not an oversight in hiding — the code says so in a comment:** *the from-date swap is unchanged, "not this task".* ⇒ **The owner's ruling (the cover is paid at the covering teacher's rate) holds for ONE session and NOT for the rest-of-series case.** 📌 **Next-round item, not this one; it is money, and it is wrong in one direction.**
2. ⚠️ **The same human event sends TWO DIFFERENT message pairs depending on the path:** **swap** enqueues `teacher_unassigned` + `teacher_assigned`; **add/remove** enqueue `other_teacher_added` / `other_teacher_removed`. 🔑 **Four kinds for one event: "who is teaching this changed."** **It matters for E, because whichever path we widen decides which words the coaches get.**

### ✅ And the answer to "could she just press one of them?"
🚫 **No.** **Control 3 does not remove anybody**, so it leaves A on the session — the exact thing she says is wrong. **Controls 1 and 2 remove exactly one person, and it is always the primary.** ⇒ **No combination of today's three controls takes a NON-primary teacher off a session and puts another on.** **Remove-then-Add is the only route, and it is two acts, two notifications and a discarded rate.**

## §2 — Sizing E: **widen Swap to pick WHO goes out**
### (a) What restricts Swap to the primary — **BOTH, and the server's is the real one**
- 🔻 **Front, twice over:** the **Swap button is rendered beside the primary's name only** (each extra gets *Remove*, never *Swap*) — `OtherSeriesModal.tsx`; and the dialog **hardcodes who leaves**: `swapBody(seriesRef, series.teacherId, to)` — **there is no "from" picker at all, and `from` is literally the series' primary.**
- 🔴 **Server:** `swapOtherSeriesTeacher` **re-checks it per row** — if the row's own teacher is not the named `from`, it refuses. ⇒ **Widening the UI alone changes nothing: every request would be refused.**
- ✅ **Permission: NO new key.** **Swap, Add and Remove already share ONE grant** — `action:calendar.booking-edit` (`route-access.ts`, `PATCH /other-series/:key/teacher`; front `seriesDoors` hands all three out from the same `edit`). **Same answer as C, for the same reason.**

### (b) Does the per-session write already support a non-primary? 🔴 **The READ does. The WRITE does not.**
- ✅ **Reading is already teacher-agnostic, and I checked rather than assumed:** **`ratesOf` merges the primary's rate and every extra's into ONE map**, and **`seriesRateOf` looks the incoming teacher's rate up in BOTH locations.** 📌 **`teachersOfBooking` is the same shape.** ⇒ **Nothing on the read side needs a second idea.**
- 🔴 **But the write is an update of `bookings.teacher_id` — the PRIMARY column.** **Taking an EXTRA off a session is a DELETE on `booking_teachers`; putting one on is an INSERT.** ⇒ 🔑 **"Swap a non-primary" is a different statement against a different table, not a different value in the same one.** *That is why I will not let it be written as one more branch inside the row loop.*
- ⚠️ **And the guarantee is weaker on that side.** **The slot index constrains `bookings.teacher_id` ONLY.** **A `booking_teachers` teacher is kept out of two places at once by an APPLICATION check (`assertAdditionalTeacherFree`) that two racing requests can both pass — the code says so in as many words.** ⇒ **A non-primary swap inherits that weaker guard. It must go THROUGH the existing check, not around it, and we state plainly that it is the weaker of the two.**

### (c) Whose rate when a NON-primary is swapped out — **confirmed from the code**
✅ **The lookup needs no change: `seriesRateOf` already finds the incoming teacher's rate whether they held it as a primary or as an extra anywhere in the series, and `RATE_REQUIRED` already refuses rather than defaulting to the outgoing teacher's.** ⭐ **That satisfies the owner's ruling as written, for the non-primary case too.**
⚠️ **What changes is only WHERE it is stored: `booking_teachers.rate_minor`, not `bookings.teacher_rate_minor`.** ⇒ **The incoming extra's rate goes on the incoming extra's own row.** 🚫 **A non-primary swap must not touch the primary's rate column** — *that would re-rate a teacher nobody asked about.*

### (d) 📏 Size — **S, one TASK, BE+FE**
**BE:** `from` may name **the primary OR an extra on the target row**; **one branch, chosen by which location `from` occupies, resolved ONCE before the row loop** — 🚫 **never two ideas of "who is on this session"**, because `teachersOfBooking` already answers it. **The extra path is delete + insert on `booking_teachers` with the rate on the new row, through `assertAdditionalTeacherFree` and `assertTeacherBookable` unchanged.** **Both paths keep the existing scope rule (one session vs from here on) and every existing refusal (`ALREADY_ON_ROW`, `SLOT_TAKEN`, `RATE_REQUIRED`).**
**FE:** **render the same Swap control beside EVERY teacher on the row, not only the primary, and send that teacher as `from`.** **One dialog, one body builder; the body builder stops hardcoding the primary.**
⚠️ **One decision inside it is a COPY decision, so it is mine:** **the dialog title today reads "สลับครูหลัก" / "Swap the primary teacher ({name})".** **It stops being true the moment this ships.** 📋 **I will draft the reword with the batch and bring it to you first.**

### (e) 🔑 What of our item 5 survives — **you are owed that line, so here it is plainly**
✅ **Most of it, and the expensive parts.** **The scope chooser (this session / from here on, with nothing pre-selected — Khwan's EARLIER complaint), the cover-rate rule and its refusal, the leave-block on a cover, the per-session write path and both DOM tests (`series-scope`, `cover-needs-key`) all stand, and E reuses every one of them.**
🔴 **What does NOT survive is item 5's CHOICE OF ACT.** **We built "add a second teacher for one session" when the act she wanted was "this person is not teaching it, that person is."** ⇒ **The machinery was right and the verb was wrong.** 📌 **"Add teacher" should STAY** — a genuine second coach on a session is a real thing the school does — **but it was never the answer to her question, and establishing which act she meant was owed BEFORE item 5 was built, not after.**
