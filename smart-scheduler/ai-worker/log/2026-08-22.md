### The trap, stated plainly so nobody stumbles into it
**`IMPORT` posts no revenue at all — by design.** So using it merely to dodge a missing price means the money the
customer actually collected **never enters the books**, and `recordSale` cannot be replayed for it later. That is
the *same* silent-wrong-money class as the 9999 idea, arriving from the other direction. The rule is about
**what actually happened**, not about convenience:
- **Money was taken before go-live / already part-way through** ⇒ `IMPORT` is correct and always was.
- **Money is being taken now** ⇒ it is a **SALE**, and it needs a price line. Do not launder it as an import.

### The middle option, and Porter's recommendation
Point the program at the **closest existing line** (`bike-skate` / `onewheel` / `balance-private` /
`balance-group`). It sells immediately, the price is a real price the owner charges rather than a placeholder, and
because `subjects.price_group` is **data**, repointing it later is **no deploy and no migration** — and it does not
touch courses already sold.

**@Sober — this belongs in SPEC-058 as a stated behaviour, not folklore:** a program may be added with a NULL price
group; it is then **bookable via import but refused for sale, loudly**. That is a supported state, and REQ-058
should say so instead of implying every program must be priced on day one.
