## ⚠️ CONTRADICTIONS

### 1. Sick-leave and the freelance ceiling — the owner reversed his own locked rule

**Not resolved by me. Both sides recorded with their dates, exactly as found.** The reversal is the owner's own, stated on the record; I am reporting it rather than folding the old side away, because the old rule is referenced as "locked" in TASK-028 and in the tests.

- **Side A — the earlier, explicitly LOCKED rule (owner, before 2026-08-03; recorded in TASK-028):** a sick-leave **still draws** the freelance ceiling — *"still pay the sick-leave freelance."* `SICK_LEAVE` sat in `FREELANCE_CONSUMING_STATUSES`, so the absent session kept its drawn hour **and** the appended make-up drew its own when taught ⇒ **one absence ate two hours** of the monthly freelance ceiling.
- **Side B — the reversal (owner, 2026-08-03):** *"SICK_LEAVE no longer draws the freelance ceiling."* `heldTarget(SICK_LEAVE) → 0`; only the make-up draws, when actually taught ⇒ **one hour, not two**. Compensation, if wanted, is a **manual** hand-entered fuel allowance (ค่าน้ำมัน), never automatic.
- **Trigger:** SA surfaced that two separately-decided correct rules *combined* to make freelancers hit their monthly cap roughly twice as fast on sick-leaves. The owner saw the combination and changed his mind.

### 2. Attended-immutability — built, then relaxed within the same day

- **Side A (spec + build, 2026-08-03):** "attended sessions can't be edited away" — `isDelivered` guard added to `updateBookingStatus:cancel`, so a mis-marked attendance could not be undone by cancel.
- **Side B (owner, 2026-08-03, same day):** an attended session **CAN** be cancelled provided a non-empty reason is supplied and stored for audit. The guard stays for edit/move only.
- Not a live contradiction (B supersedes A), but recorded because A shipped as an acceptance criterion and both halves are still true of different operations.
