## Business rules the owner set earlier and that were never written down

- **A course's expiry is COMPUTED, never taken from the form:** *expiry = start + (size + leave quota) weeks*, and **a leave never extends the expiry** (owner, 2026-08-25 — stated to Porter; the rule was enforced nowhere in the code at the time).
- **The three course sizes and their week ceilings all fall out of that one rule: size 4 → 5 weeks · size 6 → 8 weeks · size 10 → 13 weeks** (owner, 2026-08-25) — i.e. **leave quota is 1 / 2 / 3 sessions** for the three sizes. 📌 This closed a 3-week-old `lib/leave.ts:13` comment that said *"6→8 is an ASSUMPTION — confirm"*.
- **The start date IS week 1** — the ceiling counts weeks *including* the first (owner's own worked case, 2026-08-25: 6 sessions starting 4 Sep, ceiling week 8 ⇒ expiry **23 Oct**, not 30 Oct).
- 🔴 **A course has FOUR lifecycle statuses, not a binary active/inactive toggle** — the owner overruled Porter's binary: *"แยกประเภทไป ทำประเภทขึ้นมาคลุมเลย"* (owner, 2026-08-25). Precedence: **`CANCELLED` → `COMPLETED` → `EXPIRED` → `ACTIVE`**, one status per course, the four counts summing to the unfiltered total.
- **`COMPLETED` beats `EXPIRED` deliberately: a family who used everything has no problem, while expired-with-sessions-left is a family that PAID FOR CLASSES THEY NEVER RECEIVED** — the only status that costs the customer money (Porter's AC, written to follow the owner's ruling, 2026-08-25). ⚠️ A binary would have merged those two and hidden the only one that matters.
- **An ended (cancelled) course accepts no write that adds a session, revives a forfeited one, or bills; `cancel` and `sick-leave` stay ALLOWED** because they only move a session further from delivery, and blocking them would trap a row nobody can clear (team rule accepted by owner's use, 2026-08-25).
- 🔴 **A "charged extra session" (`เพิ่มคาบ (คิดเงิน)`) on a course creates a `SINGLE_SESSION`, which POSTS REVENUE at day-end once attended** — that is how a course bills a family outside its package (observed, 2026-08-25).
- 🔴 **A `CONFIRMED` session that nobody marks AUTO-ATTENDS at day-end and CONSUMES QUOTA** — REQ-070, the owner's own design; he confirmed he knows this consequence before approving bulk course-confirm (owner, acknowledged 2026-08-25).
- **The ordinary session-cancel path RE-OWES a make-up by design (SPEC-028 §11.3); ending a whole course does NOT re-owe and moves NO money** — measured before/after: `extended` 1 → 0, `size` unmoved, `bo.movement` 26 → 26 (observed on `sid`, 2026-08-25).
- **Ending a course is a SOFT cancel: sessions are MARKED `CANCELLED`, never deleted, and the leave row is preserved** — a course ended by a typo can be inspected and recovered (observed, 2026-08-25).
- **The three end reasons are `ADMIN_ERROR` · `PROGRAM_CHANGED` · `CUSTOMER_CANCELLED`** — validated server-side against that list (observed, 2026-08-25).
- **A course expiring TODAY is still `ACTIVE`** — expiry is inclusive of the day (observed, 2026-08-25).
- **A leave-locked course is still `ACTIVE`** — lock is *quota* state, lifecycle is a separate dimension, and the two render as two chips side by side, never merged (observed, 2026-08-25).
- 🔴 **The owner prioritises by "is any DATA wrong, is any MONEY wrong"** — he ruled the broken cell-display toggle *not urgent* on exactly that test, even while staring at it (owner, 2026-08-25).
