## Product limits

- 🔴 **Nothing is ever deleted anywhere in the product.** `api.ts:34` states it outright: *"Nothing is ever deleted; suspend is the off switch."* There is **no delete endpoint for vouchers, courses, students or parents**, and no hard delete of a teacher either (bookings FK) (Tanya, 2026-08-01; Jason, 2026-08-01).
- **`createVoucher` posts revenue to the `bo` money ledger, and that revenue row cannot be removed** — so creating a test voucher leaves permanent money residue (Tanya, 2026-08-01).
- 🔴 **There is one shared backoffice credential and one login for everyone; the frontoffice login does not distinguish users either.** Per-person permissions **cannot be enforced today**. Anything the customer asks for that is "only person X may do this" (e.g. board-only approval of freelance top-ups) needs **separate logins as a prerequisite**, not as a rider (Porter, 2026-08-01).
- **`backoffice-front` has NO i18n layer at all** — no `lib/i18n`; the P&L screen, the nav and Items are hardcoded Thai. Ruled: **the backoffice stays Thai-only** until someone raises EN as its own REQ (Fern + Sober, 2026-08-01).
- **`backoffice-front` has no mock mode** — its client points at the backoffice API, so FE work there is exercised against a throwaway stub server, never a real environment (Fern, 2026-08-01).
- **Subjects/programs cannot be created in the UI** (known limit since REQ-003) (Sober, 2026-08-01).
- **Leave via LINE only works for TODAY's bookings** (`doLeave` → `findTodayBookingsForParent`). **A parent cannot report tomorrow's absence through LINE at all**, despite the product promising *"การแจ้งลาล่วงหน้า"* (Porter, 2026-08-01).
- **A freelance teacher with no budget set renders exactly like one who is perfectly healthy — as nothing.** `budgetTone` returns `null` when `budgetMinor` is null or `<= 0`, and the strip returns `null`. "Not configured" and "fine" are indistinguishable on screen (Porter, diagnosed 2026-08-01).
- **A voucher is structurally unattributable to a sport** — it is generic hours, has no sport at sale, and its sessions may later be different sports. **No join and no amount of tagging at sale time fixes this** (Sober, 2026-08-01).
- **Revenue by branch / onsite-vs-online (*"ยอดตามสาขา"*) is structurally impossible** in this data model (REQ-021 finding 9, restated 2026-08-01).

- **The badge system has two known defects: badges cannot be removed or deactivated, and the badge *report* silently drops untagged rows** (Porter, restated 2026-08-01).

## LINE

- **LINE check-in requires all three of: `date = today` **and** `status = CONFIRMED` **and** the student is one of this LINE account's children.** When any of them fails the reply is the single message *"ไม่มีคาบให้เช็คอิน"*, which cannot tell the cases apart (Porter, 2026-08-01).
- **Check-in tokens expire at the end of the class** (`tokenExpiryIso(row.date, row.endTime)`) — that expiry is correct and must not be loosened (Porter, 2026-08-01).
- ✅ **The check-in window is ±30 minutes — the owner decided it:** *"เอาบวกลบ30นาที"* ⇒ open at **start − 30** and close at **end + 30** (owner คุณฟีน, 2026-08-01). Before this it opened at start − 30 (`CHECKIN_EARLY_MINUTES = 30`) and closed at `end`, so a parent arriving 45 minutes early was told they had no class.
- **`updateBookingStatus` throws on the deliberate business rules** (the advance leave-notice rule and the over-quota lock), and the LINE leave handler had no `try/catch` — so a correctly-fired rule produced **no reply at all**: *"กดแจ้งลา แล้วหายไปเลย ไม่ขยับอะไรเลย"* (owner คุณฟีน reporting; Porter diagnosing, 2026-08-01).
- **REQ-017 (the teacher calendar `.ics` feed) is blocked on a platform wall, not a decision: LINE will not linkify `webcal://`, and Google Calendar mobile cannot add a calendar by URL.** `calendarUrls()` already returns both an `https` and a `webcal` URL; only the `webcal` one is sent (Porter, 2026-08-01).
- **The bot must not become an oracle:** a request naming a real teacher and one naming nobody must look **identical** to the person typing, and the reply must not echo the nickname back. An "archived" teacher must read as *not found*, or the roster leaks to an unauthenticated stranger (Sober + Jason, 2026-08-01).
