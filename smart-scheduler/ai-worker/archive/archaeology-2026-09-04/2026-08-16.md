## Product limits

- 🔴 **The COURSE tab in the New-booking modal does a plain ADD (+1 session)**, `createBooking` → `insertBooking`. **It does NOT consume the tail, does NOT keep the course total constant, and does NOT recompute the end date** — it can over-fill a fixed-size course to `size+1` with no owed-session check and no charge (Sober, grounded in code, 2026-08-16).
- **The real make-up move lives elsewhere — the plan modal's `แทรกคาบชดเชย` (`applyPlanChange` → `reconcileCoursePlan`), and REQ-037's paid extra is `เพิ่มคาบ (คิดเงิน)`** (Sober, 2026-08-16).
