## Courses, quota and imported courses

- **`size` is about the PURCHASE.** Quota, label and expiry all key off `size`, never off how many bookings exist (observed, 2026-08-22).
- **Leave quota is derived from course size** — a 10-session course gives quota 3, a 6-session course gives quota 2 (`leaveQuota(size)`, observed 2026-08-22).
- **An imported "already in progress" course deliberately creates ONLY the remaining bookings, not the sessions already taught** — inventing past bookings would put fictional attendance into the reports. That decision is correct and must survive any fix (observed, 2026-08-22).
- **`prior_sessions` (immutable, 0 for every SALE course) is what separates "already taught before import" from running attendance.** Plan responsibility uses `size − prior_sessions`; using `size − used_sessions` instead would **cancel real future sessions of a paying family** (Sober's ruling + Jason's build, 2026-08-22).
