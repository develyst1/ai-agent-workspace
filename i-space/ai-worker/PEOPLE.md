# PEOPLE — who is who on this desk (read at boot, every role, right after SYSTEM-FACTS.md)

> **What it is:** every outside person this desk deals with — repo owners, reviewers, the lead, the customer's people —
> one row per person: how the operator says their name, their full name, their GitHub login, their e-mail handle,
> their role/team and what they own. A message, a PR body or a reviewer line is written **from this table**, never
> from memory, a git author name or a guess.
>
> **Rules:**
> - **One file, one table, never split by date or topic.** People are Knowledge every message needs; a dated topic
>   file is never read at boot, and that is how a name gets lost (workforce-protocol §8).
> - **Add a row the moment you learn a name, nickname or login** — before you send your reply. Unknown cells stay
>   empty; never fill one with a guess.
> - **Address people by the nickname column**, as the operator says it (e.g. `พี่<nickname>`). Never an English first
>   name or a login as the address. A person not in this table → ask the operator once, then add the row.
> - **GitHub login:** `gh-checked: yes` only when a desk file shows the `gh` check (cite it). A git author name or a
>   chat handle is not a login.
> - **Every cell cites its source** in the last column: `file:line` + date. Two sources disagree → keep both values,
>   mark ⚠️, and the PM settles it with the operator.
> - **Correct by striking** (`~~old~~ new`, with the new source) — never delete a row or a value.
> - Keep it small: the gate FAILs this file over 12 KB (it is read at boot by every role).
